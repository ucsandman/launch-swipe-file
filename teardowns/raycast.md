# Raycast: the documented launch-week playbook

## The launch
Raycast (YC W20), built by ex-Facebook engineers Thomas Paul Mann and Petr Nikolaev, launched on HN as a CLI-inspired desktop app to kill developer context switching. In July 2022 they ran a textbook launch week, and later launched their extensions API and store in public beta alongside a £11.3M Series A led by Accel and Coatue.
Sources: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22466994 and https://businesscloud.co.uk/news/developer-tool-raycast-launches-alongside-11-3m-series-a/

## What they did
- Posted a preview one week before launch week to build momentum (300 likes, 26 reposts on X).
- Ran five daily announcements: Community Stories, Look and Feel, Raycast for Teams, Menu Bar Commands, Community Swag.
- Matched each day to a channel: Product Hunt for Teams day (ranked #4 Product of the Day), a technical behind-the-scenes post for HN, X for everything.
- Shipped 1 changelog post, 4 blog posts, and a recap post to close the week.
- Made the API/store launch a community event: extensions other developers built became the announcement content.
  Source: https://dev.to/launchweekdev/how-to-launch-week-raycast-2hhp

## What worked
- Daily active users grew from 130 in October 2020 to over 11,000 in 12 months, with 20M+ actions performed.
- The community built 100+ extensions in the first month of the API beta.
  Source: https://businesscloud.co.uk/news/developer-tool-raycast-launches-alongside-11-3m-series-a/

## Takeaways for LegCli
- Copy the skeleton directly: preview week, five daily beats, channel per beat, recap post. It is all documented above.
- Make one beat about what the community built, not what you built. Their extensions are your launch content.
- A technical behind-the-scenes post aimed at HN earns a different, deeper audience than the Product Hunt push. Run both.
