# Supabase: launch weeks as a format

## The launch
Supabase, the open-source Firebase alternative, turned "launch week" into a repeatable format: five days, one announcement per day, each treated like a mini launch with its own blog post, X push, and community moment. Launch Week 7 ran April 10-14 with daily drops at 7AM PT / 10AM ET.
Sources: https://supabase.com/launch-week/7 and https://dev.to/fmerian/i-analyzed-how-supabase-and-laravel-launched-heres-what-i-learned-4cdd

## What they did
- Made shipping itself the show: each day got a theme, a main-stage announcement, and a build stage with community content.
- Stacked channels per announcement: blog post, X, Hacker News, Product Hunt, newsletter, Discord.
- Pulled in customers and partners per announcement to widen reach beyond their own audience.
- Ran a hackathon and prize draw alongside, so the community was building during the launch, not just watching.
- Closed every week with a recap post to keep momentum compounding into the next one.

## What worked
- Fewer than 500 GitHub stars in April 2020, 1,500 by June 2020, nearly 5,500 by December 2020; by their Series A in September 2021 they were among the fastest-growing open-source projects on GitHub.
  Source: https://www.stacksync.com/blog/one-word-changed-everything-the-origin-story-of-supabase
- The format got copied: 126 launch weeks were run by 94 different companies in 2024, per launchweek.dev.
  Source: https://dev.to/fmerian/i-analyzed-how-supabase-and-laravel-launched-heres-what-i-learned-4cdd
- Community started writing client libraries and tutorials unprompted; a retired IBM engineer became the fastest support responder in their Discord.

## Takeaways for LegCli
- A launch week beats a launch day: five small beats give you five chances to trend instead of one.
- Give each day its own artifact (blog post, demo, changelog) so every channel has something fresh to link.
- Formalize your early superfans the way the SupaSquad did: identity and thanks, not extraction.
