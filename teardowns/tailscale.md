# Tailscale: founders with receipts, tech blog first

## The launch
Tailscale launched in April 2020 as a WireGuard-based mesh VPN with a $3M seed round led by Heavybit. The founders were ex-Google engineers: Avery Pennarun (CEO), David Crawshaw (CTO), David Carney (COO), all WireGuard project contributors.
Source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200402005017/en/Founded-by-Ex-Googlers-Tailscale-Launches-to-Secure-and-Simplify-Remote-Network-Access-With-%243M

## What they did
- Published a deep technical blog post about how the product works a month BEFORE the launch announcement, so HN had substance to chew on.
  Source: https://mytechdecisions.com/network-security/tailscale-launches-new-quick-setup-vpn/
- Led with a before/after number anyone could feel: VPN setup time from weeks down to minutes, using the team's existing identity provider.
- Anchored credibility in the founders: Google Fiber, Fuchsia OS networking, WireGuard contributors. The press release reads like a resume, and that was the point.
- Named the enemy precisely: "Every company above a certain size has a VPN and every one of them hates their VPN." Universal pain, existing budget.
- Framed the mission as rebuilding the internet's backbone, which gave every later launch a story to plug into.

## What worked
- $3M seed at launch, $100M Series B in May 2022, $160M Series C later; the launch narrative (simple, secure by default) survived all three rounds unchanged.
- Years later, AI companies like Perplexity, Mistral, Cohere, Groq, and Hugging Face build on Tailscale, per the CEO.
  Source: https://www.thestack.technology/mesh-network-firm-tailscale-raises-160m-hits-10-000-customers/

## Takeaways for LegCli
- Publish the technical deep-dive before the announcement. HN rewards the explainer, not the press release.
- If your founders (or you) have receipts, put them in the launch. Credibility is a feature.
- Name the hated incumbent and the existing budget line. "Everyone hates X and already pays for X" is a launch in one sentence.
