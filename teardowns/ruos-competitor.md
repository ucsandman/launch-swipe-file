# ruOS (Cognitum One): competitor teardown

## The move
Cognitum One announced ruOS on October 6, 2026 via Business Wire: "the Enterprise Computer Platform for AI Agents." This is a competitor teardown, not a launch to copy. ruOS sells persistent task context and fleet coordination to enterprises, directly overlapping LegCli's session-persistence space.
Sources: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261006476353/en/Cognitum-One-Announces-ruOS-the-Enterprise-Computer-Platform-for-AI-Agents and https://genznewz.com/cognitum-one-launches-ruos-agent-os-for-enterprises

## What they did
- Newsjacked the category leaders. The release opens with Meta's Muse and OpenAI's dots, then reframes both as "guest applications" while "ruOS is the host." One contrast sentence does the whole positioning job.
- Anchored the future to a past transformation. Founding advisor and former Citrix CEO Mark B. Templeton: remote desktops freed workers' computers from under their desks, and "ruOS does for agents what remote desktops did for people." Borrowed credibility, zero demos needed.
- Stacked three named components for the tech story: RuFlo chains specialized agents through pipelines, RuVector keeps task context persistent so work resumes where it left off, MetaHarness evaluates agent output. Each one answers an enterprise objection: coordination, continuity, verification.
- Sold sovereignty, not features. MCP-native, ships as a .deb, runs on customer infrastructure, supports air-gapped deployments, bring your own models and assistant. The pitch is where the computer lives and who controls it. Available today at ruos.cognitum.one.

## What worked
- The "host rather than the guest" line gave every syndicated pickup the same quotable frame; Business Wire syndication carried it across dozens of outlets within 24 hours.
  Source: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261006476353/en/Cognitum-One-Announces-ruOS-the-Enterprise-Computer-Platform-for-AI-Agents
- The Citrix analogy made an unfamiliar product feel inevitable. Independent coverage led with the enterprise angle, not the tech spec.
  Source: https://genznewz.com/cognitum-one-launches-ruos-agent-os-for-enterprises
- Founder Reuven "rUv" Cohen's open-source track record (RuView, RuFlo, RuVector) was cited in the release, lending the enterprise pitch builder credibility.

## What LegCli can learn about differentiation
- The category is validated, and the overlap is real. RuVector's "persistent task context" is LegCli's session handoff described in enterprise words. Enterprises will buy this; the question is who sells it to them.
- Do not fight ruOS on its ground. It sells the whole governed computer; LegCli should sell the session layer that works inside any host, including theirs. "Works with your existing setup" beats "replace your setup" for indie reach.
- Steal the contrast-sentence tactic. ruOS has "the host rather than the guest." LegCli needs one sentence that positions against cloud agents and local CLIs alike, quotable inside someone else's launch coverage.
- Anchor to a past transformation the same way. If remote desktops freed people's computers, session handoff frees agents' work from any single machine. Say it that plainly.
